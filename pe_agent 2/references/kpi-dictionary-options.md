# KPI Dictionary Options for PE Portfolio Companies (Germany/Europe SME)

Use this as a menu. Pick one core dictionary per company, then add sector modules.

## 1) Fund and LP reporting dictionary
Best for: partner/IC/LP view.

Core KPIs:
- Gross IRR, Net IRR, TVPI, DPI, RVPI
- MOIC by deal and by fund
- Portfolio leverage (Net Debt / EBITDA)
- Covenant watchlist count (amber/red)
- Value-creation realization (% of target)

Reference examples:
- ILPA Reporting Template resources: https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/ilpa-reporting-template-v-2-0/

## 2) Financial health dictionary (all SMEs)
Best for: monthly company review.

Core KPIs:
- Revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA margin
- Free cash flow (or operating cash flow proxy)
- Net debt, Net Debt / EBITDA, Interest Cover
- Working capital: DSO, DPO, DIO, Cash Conversion Cycle
- Cash runway / liquidity buffer

Notes:
- Keep one canonical formula owner (fund controller or value-creation lead).

## 3) Industrial / manufacturing dictionary
Best for: DACH industrials and components.

Core KPIs:
- OEE, scrap rate, rework rate
- On-time in-full delivery (OTIF)
- Backlog coverage
- Throughput and cycle time

Reference examples:
- ISO 22400 (manufacturing KPI framework): https://www.iso.org/standard/54497.html

## 4) Software / subscription dictionary
Best for: software-enabled SMEs and SaaS models.

Core KPIs:
- ARR/MRR
- NRR, Gross churn, Net churn
- CAC payback, gross margin, Rule of 40
- Pipeline conversion and win rate

Reference examples:
- IFRS Sustainability (SASB) industry metrics finder: https://sasb.ifrs.org/standards/materiality-finder/

## 5) People / HR dictionary
Best for: services and talent-heavy businesses.

Core KPIs:
- Revenue per FTE, EBITDA per FTE
- Voluntary attrition
- Absence/sick days per FTE
- Hiring lead time and critical-role vacancy days

Reference examples:
- ISO 30414 Human Capital Reporting: https://www.iso.org/standard/71201.html

## 6) ESG / sustainability dictionary
Best for: lender/LP ESG requests and policy tracking.

Core KPIs:
- Scope 1/2 emissions (if material)
- Energy intensity
- Lost-time incident rate (LTIR)
- Board independence, anti-corruption training coverage
- Material topic metrics per industry

Reference examples:
- GRI Standards overview: https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/
- IFRS Sustainability (SASB): https://sasb.ifrs.org/standards/materiality-finder/
- EFRAG ESRS (EU sustainability standards): https://www.efrag.org/lab6

## Selection method (recommended)
1. Start with a 12–15 KPI base pack (financial + liquidity + leverage + one commercial + one ops + one people).
2. Add up to 8 sector KPIs only if they are decision-driving.
3. Ensure each KPI has: owner, source system, refresh cadence, and board-level decision linked.
4. Drop KPIs that do not trigger action for 2 consecutive quarters.

## Practical target counts
- IC / partner dashboard: 8–12 KPIs
- Company monthly cockpit: 15–25 KPIs
- Full dictionary in data platform: 40–80 KPIs (not all shown every month)
